## Artwork prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one premium 3D sculptural artwork for the hero of FOCUS, a dark, bold personal task manager by Almis. Subject: one beautiful abstract folded toroidal ribbon, a pair of interlocking fluid chrome loops with an amethyst translucent glass segment and subtle lavender edge light. Confident and architectural, not a toy. Ultra realistic brushed titanium, polished black chrome, soft lavender reflections, delicate surface texture, refined luxury tech product lighting. A single cohesive sculpture with depth and a strong silhouette, hovering in a dark graphite void, very subtle soft bloom and tasteful grain. Near-black charcoal background #141119, smooth and quiet at the edges so it blends into a dark interface. Square canvas, the entire sculpture visible with generous breathing room, dominant object fills about 70 percent of the frame. Product render, carefully art-directed and sophisticated. No letters, no text, no logos, no interface, no checkmarks, no people, no extra objects, no neon rainbow, no watermark. This is an image asset, not a website screenshot.
